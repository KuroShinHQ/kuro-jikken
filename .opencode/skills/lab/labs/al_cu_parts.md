another part. A part that only floats in space is not a part.

Tier: **Figure** (one mechanism) - target 40-80 named parts. This list has **52**.

## What this fixes

The previous scene had 6 parts (base, susceptor, wall, floor, melt, probe). anatomy's test is
"at 1x it reads as one machine, at 4x every region still has something true to find". Six parts
fail both halves: no feet, no frame, no coil, no controls, nothing to discover when you zoom.
